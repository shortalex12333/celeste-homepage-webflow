04Settled

Sign. Acknowledge. Notify. Sealed.

Once an outgoing engineer signs and the incoming one acknowledges, the handover is sealed and everyone who should know is notified. From that point it can't be rewritten behind your back — what you signed is what stays.

A sealed handover is the version of record. Settled, not editable.

05At the passerelle

Photograph the packing list. The discrepancy flags itself.

Crew photographs the packing list as the delivery comes aboard. It's matched against the purchase order and what inventory was expecting. When the count doesn't agree, the short line is flagged as a plain fact — before the truck has left and before anyone has signed for it.
