What no one writes down

Every vessel does one thing no one writes down. It forgets.

Each rotation, a year of hard-won knowledge walks down the gangway in one person's head. The next engineer inherits equipment, not context.

What we stand against

The enemy is the forgetting, not the system.

Every vessel runs a PMS. It schedules the work and holds the class approvals, and it should. The enemy is not the system. It is what the system was never built to hold: why this fault keeps returning, which supplier had the part, the briefing no one wrote down.

CelesteOS runs beside it and keeps what would otherwise leave on the gangway.

Why this exists

Built by someone who inherited the empty handover.

Alex Short spent years at sea as an Electro-Technical Officer, then Head of Department, on motor yachts from 70 to 125 metres: M/Y Freedom, Nero, Legend, Maryah, Whisper. He joined mid-rotation and found the knowledge already gone, reduced to a document written at 1 a.m. by someone packing to leave.

I built the system I wished had been there. It holds what the last engineer knew, so the next one doesn't start blind.
Alex Short · Founder · former superyacht engineer
The discipline

What we refuse to do.

A handover written at 1 a.m. is not a record, it is a confession. Knowledge that lives only in a person is knowledge the vessel does not own. So the discipline is mostly what we refuse to do.

  • We do not claim certification we do not hold.
  • We do not show customer logos we do not have.
  • We will not say a handover writes itself when an engineer taps once to capture it.
  • We do not replace the PMS your class society approved.
  • We will not dress plain tools in the language the rest of the category hides behind.
The long view

It doesn't reset to zero.

Most systems reset to zero every time the crew turns over. This record does the opposite: it holds more with every rotation. Not worth more in money. Worth more in use. The longer it runs, the less you can afford to lose it.

If you run a vessel

The door is open.

You do not start from zero. We import your equipment register, certificates, and work-order history; you keep working in the PMS you already run; there is no lock-in. We can only do this well a few vessels at a time, so the pilot is three to five, by selection.

The most honest thing said about it so far came from a captain watching a live demo:

"You actually built it. This is the Bitcoin in 2009."
