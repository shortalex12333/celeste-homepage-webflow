10 / proof, when proof matters

Every record independently verifiable.

HMAC + Merkle root + RFC 3161 timestamp. Five public checks, recomputable by anyone, without a CelesteOS account, without our involvement.

Why cryptographic, not just digital

"Digital" means a vendor's database says so. Cryptographic means the customer can prove it without trusting the vendor — even if the vendor is offline, compromised, or has ceased to exist. The 2023 DNV ShipManager outage left 1,000 vessels without access to their own records for two months. CelesteOS records survive that failure mode by design.

12 / the score

Every vessel gets a number. Free, all year.

A single score for operational truth — combining what the world can see from the outside with what the boat actually feels inside. Yours, always visible, never charged.

— Your score private to your vessel
Care The outside view. Yard time, refit cadence, public AIS. Observed from how the world sees the boat. Cannot be faked by the crew.
Wear The inside view. Fault frequency, fix velocity, repeats climbing. Computed only from your own data. Yours, never shared.
Delta The gap. When wear accelerates while care looks normal from outside — that is the decline nobody can see today.

Like a credit score or a Carfax report — the number is free, all year, only ever visible to you. What is sold is the verified, signed export, released by the owner to one counterparty, at one moment. Sale. Renewal. Claim.
