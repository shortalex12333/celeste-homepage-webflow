12 / the score

Every vessel gets a number. Free, all year.

A single score combining what the world can see from outside with what the boat actually feels inside. Yours, always visible, never charged. What is sold is the verified export — released at one moment, to one counterparty.

Care — the outside view, observed from public AIS and yard data. Wear — the inside view, computed from your own data: fault frequency, fix velocity, repeats climbing. The score is the delta between them.
