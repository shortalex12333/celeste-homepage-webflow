04Search

Ask in plain language. Get the answer with its source.

Ask the vessel a question the way you'd ask a crew member. Results come back citing where they came from — the manual page, the work order, the thread — for you to confirm. CelesteOS surfaces what's there; it never makes anything up, and when there's nothing, it says so.
