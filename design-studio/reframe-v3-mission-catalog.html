Positioning

CelesteOS does not replace your PMS.

Your existing PMS — Seahub, ShipManager, IDEA, smartPAL, MXSuite, or otherwise — keeps its class society approvals, its workflows, and its crew familiarity. CelesteOS indexes everything it produces, alongside your manuals, emails, work orders, and notes.

Lower switching cost. Lower implementation risk. Higher operational continuity.

Continuity & portability

The vessel's data is the vessel's data.

CelesteOS holds the vessel's record in a documented format. Every fault, work order, handover, and linked document is exportable on demand in standard formats (CSV, PDF, signed JSON). A documented escrow arrangement ensures continuity if CelesteOS the company ceases to operate. The vessel's data leaves with the vessel.
