Continuity

"What happens if you get hit by a bus?"

Customer data exports — every fault, work order, handover, and linked document — are produced on request in standard formats. A documented escrow arrangement ensures continuity if CelesteOS ceases. The vessel's data leaves with the vessel.

Coexistence

"Do we rip out IDEA?"

No. Your existing PMS keeps its class approvals, workflows, and crew familiarity. CelesteOS sits alongside, indexes what it produces, and adds the handover and search surfaces on top.

Liability

"What is signed and what is sealed?"

The handover is editable until the departing officer signs. After that, the record holds in the order it happened, attributed, and yours to export. CelesteOS does not adjudicate, does not auto-file, does not auto-send.
