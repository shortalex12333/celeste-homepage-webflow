(01) The painkiller
The handover the boat writes for itself.

The rough notes you'd bash out at 1am on your last night — dropped into CelesteOS, returned in minutes as a structured, survey-ready handover. Every entry linked to the source record: the fault, the work order, the parts used, the manual page. Editable up to sign-off. Sealed after.

The incoming engineer inherits context, not chaos.

(02) The vitamin that supports it
Search across anywhere.

Ask in plain language. CelesteOS searches across manuals, faults, parts, work orders, certificates, email, and warranty terms.

And runs targeted searches you didn't think to ask for.

(03) What it searches for, while you sleep
Five missions. Every one cited.
01 · Money

Money about to be spent.

Items still under manufacturer warranty surface the moment a fault is raised against them — with a forward-ready claim pack: fault history, photos, work order, cited warranty clause. You forward. The supplier pays.

02 · Certificates

Certificates before they expire.

Survey 60 days out. CelesteOS gathers every certificate the surveyor will ask for — and flags the three that expire inside the window — into one signed bundle.

03 · Receiving

Discrepancies on the dock.

A photo of the packing list goes in. Matched PO lines, the inventory delta, and a drafted discrepancy email come out. The shipment goes back before it's signed for.

04 · Faults

Faults already diagnosed.

The same fault, on the same equipment, three rotations ago. The diagnosis is in the record. CelesteOS surfaces it before the marine technician's callout is booked.

05 · Answers

Answers across email and manuals.

"Which generator setting did the manufacturer recommend after last summer's overheating?" — answered, with the email thread and the manual page beside the answer.

Every search above is a draft, not an action. You read the citation; you decide; CelesteOS records what you decided. It never auto-files, auto-sends, or auto-asserts.
For the office that signs
Across your fleet, every handover comes out in the same shape.

A new chief engineer joining any one of your fifteen boats is productive on day 1 instead of week 2. The operational record survives crew rotation. The first survey of every season opens without a scramble. The risk you carry as the management company is a risk that now has an audit trail behind it — yours, exportable, on demand.

Positioning
CelesteOS does not replace your PMS.

Your existing PMS — Seahub, ShipManager, IDEA, smartPAL, MXSuite — keeps its class society approvals, its workflows, and its crew familiarity. CelesteOS indexes everything it produces, alongside your manuals, emails, work orders, and notes.

Lower switching cost. Lower implementation risk. Higher operational continuity.

Continuity & portability

The vessel's data is the vessel's data.

CelesteOS holds the vessel's record in a documented format. Customer data exports — every fault, every work order, every handover, every linked document — are produced on request in standard formats. A documented escrow arrangement ensures continuity if CelesteOS the company ceases to operate. The vessel's data leaves with the vessel.
