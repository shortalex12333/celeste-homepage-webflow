CelesteOS keeps the vessel’s operational knowledge in the vessel. Every fault, every diagnosis, every decision, recorded against the vessel, not the person who made it. Every answer cites its source. Every action is yours to confirm. CelesteOS proposes; the crew decides.

Philosophy

(01)
Most systems expect crews to know where things live. That fails under pressure, fatigue, and rotation. CelesteOS removes navigation entirely. Every manual, work order, fault record, and email, searchable as a single operational record. The vessel's knowledge stays in the vessel.

Built by an ETO who lived this problem. Alex Short spent five years as an Electro-Technical Officer, then Head of Department, on superyachts up to 122 metres, including Maryah, Legend, Freedom, Nero, and Whisper. He inherited vessels mid-rotation and watched operational knowledge walk off the gangway with every handover.

CelesteOS is the system he wished existed at that moment.

What CelesteOS searches for you

Five missions. Every one cited.

The same engine as the handover, pointed at the moments where money quietly leaves the boat.

01 · Money

Money about to be spent.

Items still under manufacturer warranty surface the moment a fault is raised against them, with a forward-ready claim pack: fault history, photos, work order, cited warranty clause. You forward. The supplier pays.

02 · Certificates

Certificates before they expire.

Survey 60 days out. CelesteOS gathers every certificate the surveyor will ask for, and flags the three that expire inside the window, into one signed bundle.

03 · Receiving

Discrepancies on the dock.

A photo of the packing list goes in. Matched PO lines, the inventory delta, and a drafted discrepancy email come out. The shipment goes back before it is signed for.

04 · Faults

Faults already diagnosed.

The same fault, on the same equipment, three rotations ago. The diagnosis is in the record. CelesteOS surfaces it before the marine technician’s callout is booked.

05 · Answers

Answers across email and manuals.

“Which generator setting did the manufacturer recommend after last summer’s overheating?” Answered, with the email thread and the manual page beside the answer.

Every search above is a draft, not an action. You read the citation; you decide; CelesteOS records what you decided. It never auto-files, auto-sends, or auto-asserts.
Positioning

CelesteOS does not replace your PMS.

Your existing PMS (Seahub, ShipManager, IDEA, smartPAL, MXSuite, or otherwise) keeps its class society approvals, its workflows, and its crew familiarity. CelesteOS indexes everything it produces, alongside your manuals, emails, work orders, and notes, and seals the outputs into independently verifiable records.

Lower switching cost. Lower implementation risk. Higher audit defensibility.
