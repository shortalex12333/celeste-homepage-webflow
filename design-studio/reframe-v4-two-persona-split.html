What CelesteOS does, regardless of who's reading

Five missions. Every one cited.

The handover engine, pointed at the moments where money quietly leaves the boat.

01 · Money

Money about to be spent.

Items still under manufacturer warranty surface the moment a fault is raised. Forward-ready claim pack. You forward. The supplier pays.

02 · Certs

Certificates before they expire.

Survey 60 days out. Every certificate the surveyor will ask for — gathered, dated, flagged if it expires inside the window.

03 · Dock

Discrepancies on the dock.

A photo of the packing list goes in. Matched PO lines, inventory delta, drafted discrepancy email — out. Before it's signed for.

04 · Faults

Faults already diagnosed.

The same fault, three rotations ago. The diagnosis is in the record. Surfaced before the marine technician's callout is booked.

05 · Answers

Email + manuals.

"Which generator setting did the manufacturer recommend after last summer's overheating?" Answered, with the email and the manual page beside it.

Every search above is a draft, not an action. You read the citation; you decide; CelesteOS records what you decided. It never auto-files, auto-sends, or auto-asserts.
Positioning

CelesteOS does not replace your PMS.

Your existing PMS — Seahub, ShipManager, IDEA, smartPAL, MXSuite, or otherwise — keeps its class society approvals, its workflows, and its crew familiarity. CelesteOS indexes everything it produces, alongside your manuals, emails, work orders, and notes.

Lower switching cost. Lower implementation risk. Higher operational continuity.
