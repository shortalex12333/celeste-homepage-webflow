Strongest creative reframe · Painkiller-first · Workers / Search / Truth

The §11-L1 acceptance test, against every variant

  1. A captain reading the homepage in 90 seconds walks away able to say one sentence: "They write your handover for you, search every record on the boat, and save you money on what is already under warranty — and they never claim anything they cannot cite."
  2. The painkiller (handover) is leading.
  3. The vitamin (search) is supporting.
  4. The liability (receipts) is invisible on the sales surface.
  5. The §4 coverage rule (drafts + citations + crew confirms) is felt without being lectured.
  6. The word insurance, the phrase Integrity Score, and liability is the… framing never appear.
  7. Continuity & portability are stated — the solo-founder bus-factor objection is answered before it is asked.
