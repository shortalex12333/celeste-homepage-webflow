02How it compounds

Age is the asset. A record from two rotations ago answers a fault that lands today.

Nothing is asserted and nothing is learned. Old entries simply surface against new ones — the same equipment, the same part, the same room — because the history is dense enough to find. The longer it runs, the more it has to offer back.
